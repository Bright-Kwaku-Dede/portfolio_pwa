Unified Data Dashboard

An interactive R Shiny app that brings several small data tools together in one place. It runs on shinyapps.io, separate from this Flask site.

8 Interactive tools
1 Live data feed (Yahoo Finance)
R Shiny Built with

What's inside

💊

Disease Risk Calculator

Estimates a cardiovascular risk score from age, blood pressure, cholesterol, smoking status and BMI using a simplified point system. Educational only, not medical advice.

📈

Stock Trends

Loads daily prices from Yahoo Finance for a ticker and date range, with a price chart and summary figures.

📊

Sentiment Analysis

Scores text by counting positive and negative words from a small built-in word list. A simple lexicon approach, not a trained model.

💵

Wage Policy Simulator

Compares a chosen hourly wage with median rent in five US cities, using illustrative figures and a flat 15% tax assumption.

⚠️

History

A map of colonial-era events and a list of Black inventors. The map and chart figures are currently illustrative placeholders, not sourced data.

🧪

Starter Demos

Three small demos: a fixed-formula home price estimate, a rule-based temperature outlook, and an income-minus-expenses budget check.

Data notes

  • Live data: only the Stock Trends tab pulls live data (Yahoo Finance). It can fail if Yahoo is unavailable.
  • Illustrative data: the Wage Policy, History and Starter Demo tabs use sample or simplified numbers, not official statistics.
  • Not professional advice: the risk score and stock chart are for learning and exploration, not medical or financial decisions.
